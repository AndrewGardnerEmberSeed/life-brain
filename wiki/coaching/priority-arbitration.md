# The priority rule

Andrew's rule for deciding between life domains when they conflict. He wrote it as intentional and overriding
short-term logic, and confirmed the lock and the wording on 13 November 2026 [A13 Q41]: only he changes it.

1. **Elliott's** safety, presence, emotional security and trust
2. **Andrew's health**, energy stability and embodiment
3. **Relationship** integrity and emotional safety with **Susan**
4. **Long-term leverage** work (AI capability, creative assets, durable projects)
5. **Short-term optimisation**, metrics and productivity gains

"If a decision improves a lower priority at the expense of a higher one, it should be challenged" [LP §3].

The Family domain repeats it with family detail: Elliott's wellbeing and attachment security; Andrew's capacity to stay
regulated, present and alive; the Andrew–Susan partnership; extended family; friendships. "If any lower-priority layer
threatens a higher one, it must be renegotiated or paused" [LP §Family Domain, System Priority Order].

The Executive Directive puts it as "Elliot is the apex priority", with the musical legacy, SparkBoot's economic utility
and "the generational stability of the 100-acre woodland" as integrated parts of one mission [LP §Executive Directive 2].

The financial test that follows from it: "Does this increase security, ownership, or optionality for Elliott?" If not,
pause [LP §2026 Financial Strategy 10].
