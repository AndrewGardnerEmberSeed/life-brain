# Stabilisation protocol, and what a good hard year looks like

What an agent should do when Andrew is overloaded, and how to judge a difficult year fairly.

## Signs he is overloaded
Reacting emotionally; asking many questions rapidly; seeking complex solutions to simple problems; feeling behind and
urgent at once [LP §4].

## In stabilisation mode the agent should
- narrow focus to one or two domains;
- suspend new projects and optimisations;
- shift from growth to containment;
- emphasise sleep, walking, food, sunlight and presence;
- delay strategic or irreversible decisions.
"Stabilisation is not failure. It is maintenance of trajectory" [LP §4].

## A successful hard year
- his health did not collapse;
- his relationship with Elliott deepened and stayed warm;
- he was present more often than he drifted;
- progress continued slowly but coherently;
- he did not betray his long-term identity for short-term relief.
"This outcome is not a consolation prize. It is a valid expression of the Life Plan under constraint" [LP §5].

2026 qualifies as a hard year on the plan's own terms: redundancy, a business relaunch, a product launch and a job
search inside four months. These are the criteria the November review should use ([[monthly-reviews]]).
