# Music

Songwriting and musical legacy: the first of the five life-purpose domains, and music as "a key turning in a thousand
locks" — transmission, not validation [LP §Life Purpose 2; §Choices Musical Legacy].

## Now (13 November 2026): set aside
- **Songwriting not resumed** — "I'm focussed on Elliott and working towards replacing my salary with EmberSeed and
  SparkBoot" [A13 Q29].
- **All albums stalled** [A13 Q30]. **The band with Dave not started** [A13 Q31]. **The studio needs work to set up**
  [A13 Q33].
- The Musical Legacy and Studio scenes of the Choices are the widest gaps between scene and life
  ([[choices-document]]).

This is a deliberate trade made under the priority rule (Elliott first; leverage work fourth, music inside it), not
drift ([[priority-arbitration]]). Two things from the record are worth keeping beside it: in August Andrew called music
genuine maintenance of his mental health, not a distraction [conversation 2026-08-10 — The Sprint]; and the Life Plan
says creativity "does not flourish under constant obligation" and re-emerges when financial and time pressure drop
[LP §Entrepreneurial transition].

## The long arc
**10,000 songs in 10,000 days**: about **6,600 written** by January 2026, to finish before his mid-60s. Volume as the
path to mastery [LP §Life Purpose 2.1–2.2]. The bucket list goes further: **100 albums of original music**, "a
declaration of lifelong creative devotion" [LP §Happiness 2].
