# Music

Songwriting and musical legacy: the first of the five life-purpose domains, and music as "a key turning in a thousand
locks" — transmission, not validation [LP §Life Purpose 2; §Choices Musical Legacy].

## The long arc
**10,000 songs in 10,000 days**: about **6,600 written** by January 2026, about 4,400 to go, to finish before his
mid-60s. Volume as the path to mastery. The next 12–18 months were to accelerate, then settle to about a song a day
[LP §Life Purpose 2.1–2.2]. Current count: **Q29**. The bucket list goes further: **100 albums of original music**, "a
declaration of lifelong creative devotion" [LP §Happiness 2].

## Practice and play
- **The sacred block:** 07:45–08:30 songwriting, six days a week [LP §Mylor Weekly Rhythm].
- **January 2026:** guitar about four days a week; learning complex songs and revisiting his own material
  [LP §January review].
- **With others:** rehearsals and gigging with Dave (Tangerine Sunlights); a band "with no pressure to succeed"; open
  mics with Two Pronged Attack, silly songs as liberation [LP §Family 4.2; §Happiness 1]. Happening now: **Q31**.
- **AI as creative partner:** analysing songwriting over time (patterns, clichés, under-used techniques), vocal and
  guitar timing against his own baselines — augmenting self-awareness, never replacing authorship
  [LP §Life Purpose 2.4]. Learning the psychology of poetry feeds it ([[life-purpose]]).
- **Mental health:** in August Andrew described music as genuine maintenance of his mental health, not a distraction
  [conversation 2026-08-10 — The Sprint].

## The tension
A sober, arguably pessimistic view of human songwriting's future against AI, alongside an emotional need for it: "you
may intellectually devalue human songwriting while emotionally needing it… unresolved — but productive"
[LP §January review]. The August reading: motivation must be internally grounded because external validation is
shifting [conversation 2026-08-11 — Life Plan].
