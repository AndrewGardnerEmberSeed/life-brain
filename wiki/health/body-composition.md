## What the April analysis concluded
- **Visceral fat is the priority variable**, not total weight; it responds best to a deficit plus aerobic work.
- **Muscle preservation is a structural advantage** worth protecting as the deficit deepens (strength work, protein).
- The BMR–AMR spread (~1,145 kcal) gives room for a 500–750 kcal daily deficit.
- **Early losses aren't diagnostic** without data on deficit consistency, training and protein [conversation
  2026-04-12].

## Arithmetic worth knowing
From 104 kg to 80 kg is 24 kg. By the end of March 2027 (about 20 weeks from 13 November 2026) that is about 1.2 kg a
week, roughly ten times the April–November rate and above the ~0.5–1 kg a week usually sustainable without muscle loss.
Andrew has kept the target [A13 Q25]. The Life Plan's own coaching rules apply either way: escalate structure, not
heroics; reduce scope before increasing effort when energy is low ([[how-to-coach-andrew]]). The 2026 waypoint (high
80s or low 90s by December) is also out of reach from 104 kg in seven weeks. The thing the April analysis said was
missing — consistent deficit data — is still the missing piece.
