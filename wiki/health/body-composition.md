## What the April analysis concluded
- **Visceral fat is the priority variable**, not total weight; it responds best to a deficit plus aerobic work.
- **Muscle preservation is a structural advantage** worth protecting as the deficit deepens (strength work, protein).
- The BMR–AMR spread (~1,145 kcal) gives room for a 500–750 kcal daily deficit.
- **Early losses aren't diagnostic** without data on deficit consistency, training and protein. Those questions were
  never answered in that conversation; no protocol was settled [conversation 2026-04-12].

## Arithmetic worth knowing
From 107.4 kg to 80 kg is 27.4 kg. Reaching it by the end of March 2027 from 13 November 2026 (about 20 weeks) needs roughly 1.4 kg a week —
well above the ~0.5–1 kg a week usually sustainable without muscle loss. If the current weight is near April's, the
early-2027 date is out of reach and the target date needs resetting rather than the effort escalating (the Addendum's
rule: don't escalate ambition when energy is low). Whether that applies depends on Q25.
