# The health system

Health as "core enabling infrastructure" for every other domain: failure here degrades the rest, success compounds
it. Pursued for **capacity, agency and freedom**, not aesthetics [LP §Health 1–2]. Second in the priority rule
([[priority-arbitration]]). What Andrew actually does each week now is **Unconfirmed (Q26–Q28)**.

## The design principle
"Health success must not depend on daily motivation. It must depend on defaults, preparation, and automation."
Willpower is externalised into environments, schedules and defaults; feedback loops replace feelings
[LP §Health 2.2, 4.4].

## Baseline (January 2026)
107–110 kg against a target of 80 kg; reduced cardio, light running only; little or no strength work; inconsistent
on workdays; mornings not early enough; diet reliant on convenience food; mental health good and stable but not
deliberately trained; mobility not maintained [LP §Health 3]. Body data → [[body-composition]].

## The four subsystems
1. **Diet and metabolic health** — mostly soups from a finite, rotated menu (high vegetable, adequate protein, healthy
   fats, low glycaemic load, batch-cookable); mostly organic; high water content; **meals before 6 pm**; at least
   2 litres of water a day; a short pre-stocked list of approved convenience foods, all others "hostile to the plan";
   the kitchen as a behavioural interface; a daily caloric deficit with food records. Metrics: water, weekly weight
   trend, digestion, calorie estimates [LP §Health 6]. Status **Q27**.
2. **Fitness and cardiovascular** — progressive running; the cross-trainer as main indoor tool (needs repair); hiking
   and later cycling with Elliott; sessions diarised as non-negotiable, morning-biased, time-boxed; kit laid out the
   night before ("morning friction is treated as a design failure"); a second-hand Apple Watch for movement, heart rate
   and resting-HR trends. Metrics: sessions per week, duration, resting heart rate, VO₂ proxies [LP §Health 7].
3. **Strength, mobility and structure** — basic weights, compound movements, conservative progressive overload;
   mobility in every session; bike restoration for rides with Elliott. Strength as a longevity tool [LP §Health 8].
4. **Mental health and resilience** — from good to exceptionally resilient: structured meditation; a Chinese
   herbalist, explicitly including mental-wellbeing goals. Metrics: stress tolerance, sleep, recovery time, clarity
   [LP §Health 9]. The profile also records acupuncture and Chinese medicine with improved vitality [LP §Profile 8]; a
   regular health-practitioner cost of about $105 a month was in the August budget [financial_tracker.html].
   Practitioners now: **Q28**.

Loops: diet → energy → fitness adherence → mood → consistency; strength → confidence → agency; mental health
stabilises behaviour → diet. "Failures are signals, not moral defects." Reviewed monthly; adjusted, never abandoned
wholesale [LP §Health 10–11].
