# Psychological profile

The type frameworks Andrew uses on himself, and the February 2026 AI assessment built from colleagues' feedback.
Whether the AI's estimated scores belong in the wiki at all is **Unconfirmed (Q39)**; they are estimates by a chat model
from second-hand feedback, not a clinical or validated assessment.

## MBTI: INTJ (Andrew's own framework)

- **Ni (dominant):** deep internal models of how systems evolve; thinks in 5- and 10-year arcs; sees structural
  similarities across business, parenting, health, AI, music and geopolitics; insight as synthesis.
- **Te (auxiliary):** drives insight into plans, frameworks and metrics; comfortable with authority; frustrated by
  inefficiency and purposeless ambiguity.
- **Fi (tertiary):** strong private values — fairness, truth, protection of the vulnerable, integrity; emotion turned
  into action rather than expression; profound loyalty; betrayal not easily forgotten.
- **Se (inferior):** historically neglected physicality; swings between neglecting the body and sudden corrective
  pushes; increasingly values vitality and sensory grounding.

## Spiral Dynamics

Orange dominant (achievement, rational strategy, faith in tools); Yellow strong secondary (systems, holding
contradictions, accuracy over being right); Turquoise emerging (civilisational trajectories, self as a node in larger
systems, expressed through parenting, ethics and art); Blue and Green integrated (duty; empathy and repair). Uses value
systems instrumentally rather than defensively [LP §Profile 3]. The life-purpose section adds: watch for regression
under stress and integration under stability [LP §Life Purpose 3.2].

## Cognitive strengths

High verbal intelligence; rapid assimilation; analytical scepticism; comfortable interrogating institutional harm
(tobacco, opioids, asbestos, lead); designs layered frameworks; thinks in feedback loops and second-order effects;
learns by building [LP §Profile 4].
