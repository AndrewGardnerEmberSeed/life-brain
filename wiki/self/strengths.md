# Strengths

The Life Plan's "structural load analysis" of Andrew's strengths: twenty, each named with its risk where it has one.
Full text in the raw source.

**Core meta-strengths (the engine):** (1) unusually high integrative intelligence; (2) holds long time horizons
without losing meaning; (3) rare narrative coherence; (4) fundamentally oriented toward responsibility.

**Cognitive and creative:** (5) thinks like a systems architect; (6) high verbal and symbolic precision; (7) deep
aesthetic sensibility; (8) generates meaning under adversity.

**Emotional and relational:** (9) emotionally available and reflective as a father; (10) high relational
intentionality; (11) can love without possession; (12) capable of deep presence.

**Character and ethics:** (13) truth-oriented; (14) willing to look where others won't; (15) moral seriousness
without moralism.

**Strategic:** (16) early to structural shifts; (17) thinks non-linearly about value; (18) willing to rebuild
identity.

**Creative legacy:** (19) creates for transmission, not attention; (20) understands legacy as influence, not fame.

**Condensed — "if you internalise nothing else":** structurally intelligent; integrates meaning, systems and emotion;
tolerates long arcs; seeks truth over comfort; a genuinely good father; can architect environments and futures; capable
of deep presence; can rebuild himself consciously; early to what matters; has the raw materials for extraordinary
impact.

**The critical synthesis:** "Your weaknesses are not evidence of inadequacy. They are evidence of untrained power. Your
strengths are not soft traits. They are dangerous without discipline." The central tension: "built to be an architect
of meaning and systems, but … living like a philosopher waiting to begin."
