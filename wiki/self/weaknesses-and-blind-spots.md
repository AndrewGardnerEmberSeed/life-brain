# Weaknesses and blind spots

The Life Plan's "unflinching mirror": twenty-five weaknesses, each with its damage and blind spot. Full text in the
raw source.

**Root system:**
1. **Substitutes ideation for action** — strong at frameworks and visionary documents, weak at repetitive execution
   and closing loops; "you experience thinking as progress. It isn't."
2. **Addicted to the ideal self** — overestimates what future-you will tolerate; mistakes clarity of vision for
   inevitability.
3. **Avoids constraint** — prefers renegotiable systems, so goals drift and nothing is ever decided.
4. **Over-indexes on meaning, under-indexes on mechanics.**

**Execution and self-management:** (5) chronic incompletion; (6) inconsistent under stress; (7) weak relationship
with the body; (8) confuses insight with change.

**Psychological:** (9) avoids shame by staying abstract; (10) unresolved authority conflict; (11) oscillates between
grandiosity and self-criticism; (12) delays pleasure as a future reward.

**Relationships:** (13) under-invests in adult friendships; (14) the illusion of self-sufficiency; (15) risks making
his child a meaning anchor ("love can become load-bearing without intention").

**Work, money, strategy:** (16) overestimates future income stability; (17) avoids looking directly at spending; (18)
slow to ship; (19) mistakes intelligence for competitive advantage.

**Existential:** (20) oriented to becoming, not being; (21) fears wasting potential more than wasting time; (22)
hasn't fully accepted his actual constraints; (23) conflates depth with difficulty; (24) avoids irreversible choices;
(25) believes his best work is still ahead.

**The most damaging, condensed:** thinking is not progress; meaning does not create discipline; flexibility is your
enemy; constraint will save you; your body is the bottleneck; incomplete systems drain more energy than no systems;
future-you is not more virtuous than present-you; you need exposure, not insight; you are under-leveraging
accountability; you are closer to stagnation than you think — "not because you're incapable, but because you're
uncommitted."

- **(18) slow to ship** has counter-evidence since August: a SparkBoot beta with real testers, EmberSeed's public
