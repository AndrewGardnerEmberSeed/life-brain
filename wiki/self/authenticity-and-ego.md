# Authenticity and ego: the February 2026 coaching

The developmental work Andrew began in February 2026 with business coach Wayne Krueger. Whether it continued is
**Unconfirmed (Q40)**.

## Wayne's starting point
Maximising strengths is "a young man's game"; real forward movement comes from working on weaknesses. Questions he
set: am I willing to explore my dark side (Andrew named arrogance, sanctimony, conceit, contempt, passive
aggression)? What am I saying, doing or being that contributes to my exclusion from the core group? He suggested
interviewing peers on the best and worst things about him [LP §Professional Business Coaching].

## What colleagues said (Powerfleet, February 2026)
- **A direct report:** wants people in *his* conversation and suppresses opposing or negative views; makes each person
  feel most important until he says it to everyone; doesn't always go in to bat for people who deserve it.
- **A peer (Lee-Anne):** the data-driven style misses the real story; too much grace can foster a victim mindset in the
  team; once decided, he stops hearing other views; learn to mirror the person in front of you.
- **A manager one level up:** enthusiasm about his own strengths lands as arrogance; misses cues to let people exit;
  sometimes inflates a scenario's drama to be the hero who settles it; stop giving yourself kudos so others can.
- **His manager:** "No veneer. Wear a polo"; read the room for the one thing each person wants; cut to the chase —
  elaborate language as a defence mechanism (Andrew agreed).

## The session of 26 February 2026
- **Genesis pattern:** a school incident about writing between the lines, where the teacher, his home-room teacher
  and his father all sided against him — betrayal, humiliation, the need to be understood. Now: when unsafe he
  over-explains and performs to secure recognition; when safe he seeks connection.
- **Strategic shift:** actively undersell; lead Socratically ("What do you need from us right now?"); read the room
  through questions — "the man who is deeply confident and asks the question that exposes the next necessary step."
- **Authenticity as practice:** embodied, built by repetition. Somatic check: **if his throat tightens, he is managing
  perception.**
- **Motivation:** change because he sees its value, not because others demand it. The decisive cost of not changing is
  Elliott: absorbing inauthenticity, mistrusting him, learning manipulation. "My inauthenticity harms my son. This is
  unacceptable."
- **Implementation:** no announcement of a "new Andrew"; a quiet commitment — "Please tap me on the shoulder if I am
  not [authentic]."
- **Core fear:** "If I reveal who I really am, someone will see weakness." Counter with humour about his own
  idiosyncrasies.
- **Identity integration:** always analytical, always synthesising. Stress expression: over-explaining, dominating the
  narrative, inflating intensity. Healthy expression: **precise, curious, understated, direct.**
- **Shadow warning:** authenticity can be weaponised; feigned vulnerability exposed is the worst outcome.
- **Core insight:** authenticity "is congruence between thought, emotion, body, speech, action. Without performance."
- A writing assignment was set: define authenticity, its behaviours, risks, and how Elliott will experience it.

## The AI's 90-day recalibration protocol (February 2026)
1. **50% reduction** of words, context, adjectives, intensity and self-reference.
2. **Speak last:** one clarifying question, summarise others, then a view in under 90 seconds.
3. **Zero self-validation** for 90 days.
4. **No hero construction:** a 5/10 problem stays 5/10; credit others publicly.
5. **Adaptive mirroring** before each 1:1: what drives them, their language, pace, focus.
6. **The polo rule:** reduce signalling in dress, language and posture.
Plus a weekly shadow review (where did I compete, inflate, over-talk, seek validation, miss a cue?) and the formula
"gravitas = low ego display, precision, brevity, calm, status awareness, emotional containment" [LP §Report from AI].
