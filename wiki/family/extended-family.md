# Extended family

The people the Life Plan treats as continuity across time, buffering stress and reinforcing identity
[LP §Family 3]. Names are as the Life Plan spells them; two spellings conflict (Q21).

- **Laren and Hara** *(also written "Loren")* — aligned adults in Elliott's wider support system: fortnightly
  catch-ups, shared advocacy for Elliott, and an ongoing crypto investment conversation. Andrew wanted to introduce them
  to the game Pandemic [LP §Family 3.1; §Happiness 1].
- **Jen and Freya** — relationship underdeveloped through inertia, not conflict; to be built by predictable, booked
  time; consistency before depth [LP §Family 3.2].
- **Doug and Jennifer** — elders, mentors and wise mirrors: scheduled time, access to their perspective; long-arc
  stabilisers. Doug is on the snow-skiing wish list with Elliott, Susan and Ellie [LP §Family 3.3; §Happiness 4].
  Whether this is the same Doug Andrew works with on ARCA is **Unconfirmed (Q23)**.
- **Mum** — advanced age, declining health, chronic discomfort, a strong bond with Elliott. Intent: warmth and dignity
  without emotional erosion; find shared interests; reduce complaint-dominant loops; compassion with boundaries;
  manage exposure rather than suppress irritation; don't try to fix ageing [LP §Family 3.4]. Sunday evenings included
  reviewing "your mother's care schedule" [LP §Mylor Weekly Rhythm]. How she is now: **Unconfirmed (Q22)**.
- **His father** — the Life Plan records Andrew actively countering intergenerational patterns from that relationship
  [LP §Profile 9], and the school incident in which his father sided against him ([[authenticity-and-ego]]).
