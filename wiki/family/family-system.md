# The family system

How the Life Plan treats family as a whole: a living system whose health depends "less on isolated moments of
excellence and more on reliable emotional availability, predictable repair, and sustained aliveness." Andrew's role is
not to optimise outcomes but to design and inhabit the conditions in which the people he loves thrive
[LP §Family, Overarching Frame].

**It prioritises:** presence over performance, stability over intensity, curiosity over control, repair over
righteousness. **Every family decision is tested by:** "Does this increase long-term safety, vitality, and trust in the
system?" **Order:** Elliott → Andrew's own regulation → the partnership with Susan → extended family → friendships
[LP §Family]. → [[priority-arbitration]]

- **Ellie (18)** — to be a safe, empowering adult presence without intrusion: autonomy, listening, support without

- [[extended-family]] and [[friends]].

## The divorce
The Life Plan names **completing the divorce from Alicia** as a gateway task: peaceful, low-conflict, cost-controlled,
no unnecessary legal escalation, the value being "energetic and psychological closure, not victory." Many future family
configurations wait on it [LP §Family 2.1.1]. Its 2025 effects: net worth lower "due to divorce, buyouts,
emergencies"; child support of $800 a month, never missed [LP §Profile 7; financial_tracker.html].

On 19 October 2026 Andrew became director and secretary of Wilderness Properties Pty Ltd, the trustee of the Gardner
Family Trust, and the same form recorded **Allesia Jane Gardner** ceasing in both roles [emberseed-brain,
legal/entity-and-names.md]. Whether that is the person the Life Plan calls Alicia, and whether the divorce is now
complete, is **Unconfirmed (Q20)**.

## Guidance the plan gives an AI
Treat Elliott's wellbeing as the primary constraint; monitor Andrew's nervous-system load; detect avoidance masked as
patience; encourage structure where drift occurs and play where seriousness dominates; support repair conversations;
ask "What would increase aliveness here?" [LP §Family, Meta-guidance].
