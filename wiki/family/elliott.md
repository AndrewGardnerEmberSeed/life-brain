## The intent
Elliott grows up "feeling safe in himself, confident in relationship, and curious about the world" — not rushed,
hardened or optimised early. Andrew wants to be remembered as "the person who really saw me, backed me, and helped me
become myself" [LP §Family 1.1].

**Thriving, operationalised** [LP §Family 1.2]: emotionally — strong feelings without overwhelm, quick recovery, trust
that adults help make sense of feelings; physically — health issues resolved or improving, confident joyful movement,
sleep evolving toward independence; cognitively — benchmarks exceeded without coercion, curiosity-driven learning,
free questions and invented explanations; relationally — seeks Andrew voluntarily, narrates his inner world without
fear, sees adults as allies.

**Andrew must not:** use Elliott to regulate his own stress; confuse teaching with bonding; push independence before
safety; treat good behaviour as a proxy for wellbeing. **He must:** regulate himself first; listen longer than he
instructs; repair ruptures explicitly; shield Elliott from adult emotional spillover [LP §Family 1.3].

**Attachment:** Andrew as secure base, safe harbour and interpreter of experience. The signal to watch: does Elliott
move away with confidence and return easily? [LP §Family 1.4]

**Ages 4–12 — infrastructure, not harvest:** nature (weather, terrain, risk within safety); technology as a creative
tool with explanation; language for feelings and repair; agency scaled to capacity; imagination — stories, pretend
worlds, making things [LP §Family 1.5].

**Failure modes:** compliant but dull; hides negative feelings; Andrew becomes manager instead of father;
over-structuring in response to anxiety. "If detected, slow down immediately" [LP §Family 1.6]. And from the
weaknesses list: the risk of making him a meaning anchor — "love can become load-bearing without intention"
([[weaknesses-and-blind-spots]]).

## Year by year (the five-year plan)
- **2026 (≈3.5–4.3):** low-friction rituals of presence that survive work pressure — reading, outdoor movement,
  practical play, calm handovers. Watch transition tolerance, recovery after upset, language, imaginative range,
  whether he brings Andrew his ideas.
- **2027 (≈4.5–5.3, kindy and reception):** group competence without loss of warmth; at most one structured
  extracurricular path, and only for his confidence or joy.
- **2028–2029 (≈5.5–7.3):** from milestones to capability support — reading, making, music, humour, outdoor life,
  hobbies; then character through tone, modelling and exposure.
- **2030–2031 (≈7.5+):** confidence, curiosity, self-direction; secure individuation.
