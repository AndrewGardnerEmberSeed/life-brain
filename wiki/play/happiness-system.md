# The happiness system

Happiness as "a primary stabilising system… not ornamental, compensatory, or secondary to achievement." Andrew can carry
high responsibility for long periods, and the risk is that life "quietly narrow[s] into obligation, management, and
endurance." It is also intergenerational: he wants Elliott to experience him as a source of fun, curiosity, imagination
and adventure [LP §Happiness, Foundational Intent].

**Treat it as** a signal (joy present or absent), a practice, a memory factory (what Elliott will remember) and a guard
against burnout and emotional flattening.

**Principles:** joy scheduled loosely but protected fiercely ("if happiness is left to 'when there's time,' it will be
displaced by competence"); fun needs no justification; shared joy beats solo joy; small and frequent beats rare and
grand; imperfect joy counts [LP §Happiness, Structural Principles].

## Fun
Monday date night with Susan (rhythm, ease, not logistics); games nights with Susan, Simon and Ainsley; the cinema more
often; Friday drinks at Mylor; with Elliott — gaming, hiking, building, making things up; music as fun — a no-pressure
band, open mics with Two Pronged Attack, silly songs; seeding games like Pandemic with Laren and Hara.
**Intervene if** weeks pass without laughter, all activities become "useful", or Elliott receives care but not play
[LP §Happiness 1].

## Bucket list (deliberately incomplete and slightly impractical)
100 albums of original music; treasure hunting with Elliott over 1,000 times; building SparkBoot "as an expression of
agency in a changing world"; a 50+ acre rural retreat; the Clare caravan site as a father–son base; becoming a
multimillionaire, mainly to unlock time, freedom, generosity and calm. No timelines needed; desire itself is data
[LP §Happiness 2].

## Travel
Local counts fully: snow skiing with Elliott, Doug, Susan and Ellie (time-sensitive); the zoo; Cleland; autumn in
Clare; camping as Elliott grows. International when money and logistics allow; possibly music-enabled. Watch how
quickly Andrew and Elliott re-regulate after a trip [LP §Happiness 4]. The Choices scene: a plane lifting off, Elliott's
hand in his, Susan's smile in the window — freedom "earned in quiet, persistent days" ([[choices-document]]).

## Health indicators

"storied, not merely managed." When happiness thins, act early — especially between father and son [LP §Happiness,
