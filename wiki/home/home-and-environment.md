not by intelligence or values but by **friction, clutter, noise and misaligned context**, so environment design is a
first-order intervention [LP §Environment 1].

## How environment works on him (for an agent)
- **Causal chains:** messy car → shame → irritation → sharper tone with Elliott; unfinished spaces → self-criticism →
  lower creative confidence → procrastination; fragmented data → rumination → overwork. And the reverse when ordered.
- **Heuristics:** irritability → inspect the car; low creativity → the studio; anxiety → data visibility; shame →
  unfinished spaces; fatigue → friction, not motivation.
- **Drift signals:** objects without homes; temporary fixes becoming permanent; searching for things; mild dread
  entering a space; not wanting to invite people in.
- **Minimum viable reset:** don't reorganise everything; restore one high-visibility surface; remove one category of
  friction; stop at competence, not perfection.
- **Loops:** daily (car rubbish, desk, tools); weekly (car check, studio scan, storage); quarterly (does the environment
  still fit; remove objects tied to expired goals; fix one lingering defect).
- **Trade-offs:** structure over cosmetics; order over decoration; storage over furniture; repairs over replacement.
- **Identity:** builder not consumer; steward not accumulator; craftsman not improviser; "father who prepares, not
  reacts." "Order is kindness to the future self."
[LP §Environment 6–13]

One of the plan's heuristics applies directly now: with the studio not set up, "low creativity → inspect the studio"
is also the cheapest step back toward music, since setting it up costs time rather than money ([[music]]).
