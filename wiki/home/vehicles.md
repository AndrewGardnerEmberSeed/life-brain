# Vehicles

The car as a transitional space between home and work, fatherhood and productivity — and for Elliott, a ritual space
and an early model of order. "Car order is not cosmetic. It is a behavioral amplifier" [LP §Environment 3.1].

## BMW X3 (2005, E83, M54 engine) — the long-term anchor
**January 2026:** about 180,000 km, mechanically sound; minor oil and roof leaks; cosmetic wear; brakes due mid-year.
Kept for all-wheel drive (fits the rural future), reasonable running costs, known history, a trusted mobile mechanic.
Plans: fix the oil leak and a failed globe, plan brakes, professional clean, headlight restoration, custom rear drawers,
a two-tier backpack system (Andrew's tools and survival kit; Elliott's supplies) [LP §Environment 3.2].
**Since:** a cylinder-5 misfire diagnosed (P0305, likely a coil pack); correct oil BMW LL-01 (Nulon full synthetic
5W-40) and power-steering fluid Pentosin CHF 11S; an AAMI insurance claim after an accident with complex fault analysis
[Claude memory]. Insurance with Bingle ($22 a month) in the August budget; rego about $724 twice a year
[financial_tracker.html]. Still on the road: **Q34**.
