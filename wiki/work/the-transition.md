# The transition: from employment to self-direction

The Life Plan's keystone: moving from salaried work into sustainable entrepreneurship that gives **time sovereignty,
location flexibility, creative breathing space and significantly higher income**. "Not merely a career change; it is
the load-bearing pillar upon which fatherhood, health, creativity, property acquisition, and psychological stability
depend" [LP §Entrepreneurial transition, Core Intent]. Since the redundancy, it is no longer a plan but the situation.

## Why (the non-negotiable drivers)
1. **Fatherhood and time sovereignty** — working in intense bursts around Elliott's rhythms; psychologically available,
   not just present. "Any entrepreneurial path that recreates rigid hours, chronic stress, or constant urgency is
   considered a failure mode, even if financially successful."
2. **Money as freedom** — property, a stable home and studio, less financial anxiety, the ability to say no. "Money is
   framed as stored agency."
3. **Creative breathing space** — songwriting re-emerges when pressure drops.
[LP §Deeper Motivations]

**Identity:** builder of systems, digital architect, pattern synthesiser, calm high-agency operator. "Any plan that
treats him as a hustle-driven, constantly-on operator will eventually collapse" [LP §Identity-Level Framing].

## Known failure patterns, and the requirements
Over-conceptualising; employment eating the best hours; over-commitment across parallel goals; loss of self-trust after
stalls ("the environment was misconfigured… not the person"). Requirements: **ruthless focus — one primary business
objective at a time**; protected deep-work blocks and fewer open loops; progress measured in shipped artefacts and
external feedback, weekly; psychological safety about money, past stalls, age and time [LP §Known Failure Patterns;
§Strategic Requirements].

## The constraint on it
EmberSeed's contracted revenue covers roughly a fifth of current spending, and spendable cash runs out around May 2027
on that basis [Financial Overview, 6 Nov 2026] → [[budget-and-runway]]. The dashboard's own reading: the contract "buys
weeks of runway, not a solution"; breakeven needs about three more retainer clients like Drafting Solutions, with
spending back to cap. So the transition now turns on two numbers that live in the dashboard: clients won, and monthly
burn.
