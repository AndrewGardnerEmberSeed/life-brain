# Powerfleet

Andrew's employer from January 2024 (Fleet Complete, then Powerfleet after the acquisition), until a redundancy in
2026. The final dates and payout are **Unconfirmed (Q01)**.

## The role (as at early 2026)
Head of Customer Success for Australia (title **Q04**); $150,000 plus a $15,000 bonus; about 100 strategic clients
(previously 30–40); a lean team of about 5.8–7.8 FTE, materially under-resourced; low churn and strong sentiment
despite it; frustration with executive disengagement and headcount refusals [LP §Profile 5]. National Leader of the
Year 2025 [LP §Résumé]. Achievements → [[career-history]].

The January 2026 review called it "a cash-flow bridge, not a growth platform", with "moral injury: knowing what good
looks like and being structurally prevented from delivering it", and a risk of "burnout-by-attrition"
[LP §January 2026 review]. The February target was a one-page capacity declaration to stop silently carrying
impossible expectations [LP §February targets 3].

## The redundancy
- **August 2026:** Andrew's role was being made redundant with two or three others in the Adelaide office, as the GM
  wanted senior leaders in his own office. He expected about $50,000 take-home (11 weeks' pay, 8 weeks ex gratia,
  3 weeks' annual leave), taxed concessionally because he turned 55 on 21 August [conversation 2026-08-11 — Life Plan].
- A possible **global AI-transformation role** for customer success (Andrew would ask $200,000) was judged unlikely:
  probably no budget behind it. The planning assumption was work through August and September, redundancy in October
  [conversation 2026-08-10 — The Sprint]. A **contracting arrangement** with Powerfleet was also being explored, with a
  rule to treat it as $0 if not confirmed by 31 August [financial_tracker.html, Rules]. Outcomes **Q02**.
- **The budget** assumed salary through September and about **$40,000 net redundancy plus $4,235 pro-rata salary in
  October** [financial_tracker.html]. → [[budget-and-runway]]
- **By September 2026** Andrew had left Powerfleet [Claude memory].
