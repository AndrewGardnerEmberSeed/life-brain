# Powerfleet

Andrew's employer from January 2024 (Fleet Complete, then Powerfleet after the acquisition), until he was retrenched
in 2026 [A13 Q04].

## The role
Regional Head of Customer Success [A13 Q04]; $150,000 plus a $15,000 bonus; about 100 strategic clients (previously
30–40); a lean team of about 5.8–7.8 FTE, materially under-resourced; low churn and strong sentiment despite it;
frustration with executive disengagement and headcount refusals [LP §Profile 5]. National Leader of the Year 2025
[LP §Résumé]. Achievements → [[career-history]].

The January 2026 review called it "a cash-flow bridge, not a growth platform", with "moral injury: knowing what good
looks like and being structurally prevented from delivering it", and a risk of "burnout-by-attrition"
[LP §January 2026 review].

## The redundancy
- **August 2026:** the role was being made redundant with two or three others in the Adelaide office. Andrew expected
  about $50,000 take-home (11 weeks' pay, 8 weeks ex gratia, 3 weeks' leave), taxed concessionally after his 55th
  birthday on 21 August [conversation 2026-08-11 — Life Plan]. A possible global AI-transformation role and a
  contracting arrangement were both on the table [conversation 2026-08-10 — The Sprint]; **both closed** [A13 Q02].
- **The payout:** it arrived as planned [A13 Q01]. Final pay was received on **10 September 2026: $49,016.79** — the
  $27,200 tax-free genuine-redundancy amount, an $18,376.94 ETP taxed at 32%, annual leave, and two final working days,
  7–8 September [Financial Overview, 6 Nov 2026]. Andrew doesn't remember his exact last day [A13 Q01]; the payslip
  points to 8 September.
- **Tax:** Andrew regards it as resolved and will deal with any later implications if they arise [A13 Q12].
