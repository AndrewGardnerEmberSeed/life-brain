# The five-year plan, 2026–2031

The Life Plan's year-by-year roadmap, organised as context, required actions and expected outcomes for each year
[LP §5 Year Plan]. Its baseline (Andrew 55, Elliott ~3.5, salary ~$150k, net worth ~$25k, ~107 kg, SparkBoot nearing
MVP) is **partly superseded**: see the note at the end.

## The frame
Read as a systems document, not a wish list. The failure mode is misalignment under load; the drifts are abstraction and
generosity without containment. So the five years are "not mainly about inspiration" but about turning a high-insight,
overloaded system into one that produces **durable facts**: Elliott securely attached and increasingly self-directed;
**80 kg by early 2027**; stronger net worth and housing optionality; real AI-linked leverage; an accumulating music
catalogue; a home that supports rather than taxes. The external backdrop — AI compressing office work, a slower,
inflation-sensitive economy (RBA), an ageing population, renewed measles risk, Taiwan risk — reduces the margin for
drift [LP §5 Year Plan, Core context].

**One function per year:** 2026 stabilisation and proof · 2027 bodily transformation completed, visible traction ·
2028 ownership and consolidation · 2029 compounding · 2030 inhabiting the architecture · 2031 the Choices document
materially descriptive, not aspirational.

## 2026 — stabilisation and proof
- **Elliott:** low-friction rituals of presence that survive work pressure.
- **Body:** ~107 kg → high 80s / low 90s by year end, through steady movement, strength, sleep and less food chaos.
- **Money and leverage:** full visibility, less leakage; net worth $40–50k; **SparkBoot must leave private
  architecture for public contact** — "the year fails if SparkBoot is still mainly elegant internal architecture by
  December."
- **Music, relationship, environment:** Alkaline and Caravan as live work; steadier tone with Susan, included in a real
  future; a friction-reduction year at home.
- **Outcome:** the year "should look governed."
Scored against reality: [[2026-scorecard]].

## 2027 — the body target completes, traction becomes visible
Elliott (≈4.5–5.3) into kindy and reception: group competence without loss of warmth, one extracurricular path at most.
**80 kg early in the year**, then maintenance. Salary toward $180k, savings $50–70k, net worth $70–100k (directional).
AI relevance must become **economically credible**, through SparkBoot, internal AI capability or a role shift. Music from
activity to continuity. With Susan, "visible shared architecture": future planning, home, money realism.

## 2028–2029 — ownership and compounding
2028: Elliott (≈5.5–6.3) — capability support; health as maintenance under load; **serious movement on housing**;
SparkBoot must answer "engine or symbol?"; separation from a pure customer-success identity. 2029: Elliott (≈6.5–7.3) —
character through tone and modelling; good cash flow into durable assets; home from "functional enough" to
"structurally ours"; leverage measured in users, revenue, authority or durable output.

## 2030–2031 — inhabiting it
2030: preservation, not rescue; "recognisably living inside parts of the Choices document." 2031, the first audit year:
Elliott thriving and self-directed; 80 kg reached in early 2027 and held; Susan part of a real foundation; home and
studio supporting life and work; money calmer and stronger; AI-era leverage real; music as "organised, accumulating
transmission." The Life Plan then "stops functioning primarily as an interpretive framework and starts functioning as a
factual description of the life being lived."
