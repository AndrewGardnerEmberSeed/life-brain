# Spending patterns

What the statements show about how money leaks, from four analyses: 2025, February 2026, fourteen months to July 2026,
and September–October 2026. The Life Plan's diagnosis: the risk is "not frivolity — it is generosity without
containment" [LP §Financial Strategy 5.3].

## Known overspend channels
On Elliott (toys, experiences), on partner experiences (dining, outings), on team generosity (coffee, lunches)
[LP §Profile 7]; and convenience spending that "buys down internal pressure while weakening structure"
[LP §Five-Year Plan 2026].

## 2025: why the $20,000 savings goal was missed
About $15–18k a year in convenience spending — "living promotion to promotion." The cuts identified: daily bought
lunch (~$4,570 a year), daily coffee (~$3,000), credit-card gambling triggering cash-advance fees (~$1,200), impulse
retail (~$5,000), and tech subscriptions (~$1,500) — about $15,270 in all [LP §2025 evaluation].

## February 2026
Net salary $8,470; net surplus **$22**. Credit-card spend $4,869, including about $825 on web and business services,
large supermarket shops alongside daily bought lunches and fast food, and a Western Australia trip. A payday card
repayment overdrew the account by $2,610; a $1 bet cost a $3.50 fee [LP §February 2026 Financial Diagnostic].

## Fourteen months to July 2026
The card never cleared in full (balance drifting from $1.8k to $4.3k); the transaction account closing between $390 and
$1,508 for twelve straight months; vehicle costs spiky; groceries and dining both high; **child support never missed**
[financial_tracker.html, Trends].

## September–October 2026 (after the redundancy)
Card purchases $5,103 and $8,519, averaging $6,811 a month. **Work lunches and gambling went to zero.** Everything
else went the other way: groceries about $1,370 a month against a $500 cap, dining and alcohol $991 against $300, fuel
and vehicle $737 against $250, retail $551 against $400 — about **$2,261 a month above plan**, "the same pattern in
both statements" [Financial Overview, 6 Nov 2026].

## Status, 13 November 2026
The card carries bills and is cleared every couple of weeks; no betting [A13 Q08]. The two habits the Life Plan named
first (bought lunches, gambling fees) are gone. The leak has moved to food and dining at home and to fuel, which fits
the drift pattern "spending money to relieve internal tension" more than any single bad habit ([[drift-patterns]]).
