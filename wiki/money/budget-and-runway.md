## The rules set in August
- **Hard stops:** no gambling on the credit card; no bought lunches or café habit on non-commute days; a 48-hour wait
  on impulse retail.
- **Credit card:** clear in full from the HISA once August salary clears (ending ~$107 a month interest); log every
  transaction within 24 hours; stop discretionary spend if the balance nears $500 mid-month; no cash advances.
- **Income conversion:** Powerfleet contracting counts as $0 unless confirmed by 31 August; CastOffCo equity isn't
  income until cash arrives; SparkBoot isn't income until $500 in subscriptions; no contract by 1 October means a
  full-time search as work hours.
- **New income:** 50% of net to rebuilding the HISA before the operating account.
- **Life Plan alignment:** Elliott's experiences between $100 and $200 a month ("presence is time, not money"); date
  nights inside the dining cap; no gym spend; no new music gear until income is stable.

## Scenarios (as drawn in August)
