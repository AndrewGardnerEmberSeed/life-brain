# Side projects

The making and tinkering outside the main ventures: where, in the Life Plan's words, "happiness becomes physical"
([[happiness-system]]). Its advice: one or two active projects at a time; unfinished work is normal; involve Elliott
where safe [LP §Happiness 3].

## Hardware
- **Cyberdeck MK1 (PrepperPi)** — a briefcase offline field computer on a Raspberry Pi Zero W: Wi-Fi hotspot, offline

  topographic maps, celestial navigation, and a skills library for a young person [Claude memory].
- **Cyberdeck MK2 (Claude Terminal)** — a Claude voice/text terminal in a Macintosh Classic case (or a 3D-printed

- **Desk and home devices built for the businesses:** SparkBoot's fridge monitor and iPhone monitor [sparkboot-brain];
