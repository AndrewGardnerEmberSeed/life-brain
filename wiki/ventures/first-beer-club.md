# First Beer Club

Two things share the name: a pub-membership product, and the companies (First Beer Club Holdings Pty Ltd and First
Beer Club Trading Pty Ltd) that Andrew directs with David Farrugia.

## The product (the 2026 plan)
A membership app giving members their **first beer free each day** at participating pubs, so pubs get predictable
repeat visits. The 2026 version was a **structured market re-test**, not a growth startup: Adelaide only, 10–20
independent pubs, 200–500 members; a restrained app rebuild (member redemption by time-limited code, a staff
validation screen, founder admin); modest member subscriptions, venues free during the test; RSA compliance and age
verification; explicit time caps; Andrew on strategy and technology, David on operations and venues; Simon had left
the founding group [LP §First Beer Club 1–12].

**Decision gates:** at the end of the test — shut down, maintain small, or prepare a larger rebuild; "no indefinite
continuation without evidence" [LP §FBC 13]. **The AI's instructions:** a bounded experiment, a source of learning not
identity; prevent scope creep, challenge sunk-cost reasoning, encourage shutdown if misaligned [LP §FBC Appendix A].
In the transition plan it was secondary and must not compete with SparkBoot [LP §Entrepreneurial transition].

**What happened:** January — no material motion; February's momentum win was to clear one external dependency (app
access, payment flow, or a first meeting) [LP §January review; §February targets]. Since then: **Unconfirmed (Q13)** —
no source in reach records a test, a gate decision, or a shutdown.
