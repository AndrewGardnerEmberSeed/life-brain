# First Beer Club

Two things share the name: a pub-membership product, now **paused**, and the companies (First Beer Club Holdings Pty
Ltd and First Beer Club Trading Pty Ltd) that Andrew directs with David Farrugia, which are "currently" the vehicles for
SparkBoot and EmberSeed [A13 Q13].

## The product (paused)
A membership app giving members their **first beer free each day** at participating pubs, so pubs get predictable
repeat visits. The 2026 version was a **structured market re-test**: Adelaide only, 10–20 independent pubs, 200–500
members; a restrained app rebuild; modest member subscriptions, venues free during the test; RSA compliance and age
verification; explicit time caps; Andrew on strategy and technology, David on operations and venues; Simon had left
the founding group [LP §First Beer Club 1–12].

**Decision gates:** shut down, maintain small, or prepare a larger rebuild; "no indefinite continuation without
evidence" [LP §FBC 13]. **The AI's instructions:** a bounded experiment, a source of learning not identity; prevent
scope creep, challenge sunk-cost reasoning, encourage shutdown if misaligned [LP §FBC Appendix A].

**Status, 13 November 2026: paused** [A13 Q13]. The test was never run, so no gate has been reached. Paused is not one
of the plan's three outcomes; the plan's own rule says to revisit it before it becomes indefinite.
