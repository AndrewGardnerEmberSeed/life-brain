# SparkBoot

SparkBoot as part of Andrew's life: since 13 November 2026, **second** of his income bets — "a side bet, but an
important one for the future" [A13 Q05, Q17]. **The business itself lives in `AndrewGardnerEmberSeed/sparkboot-brain`,
which is authoritative**; start at its `wiki/index.md` and `wiki/operations/where-we-are.md`.

## What it is
An app that helps capable people displaced or disrupted by AI move from employment toward ownership: tailored startup
ideas, then a marketplace where people build or join each other's ideas as co-owners, with progress visible.
Positioned *before* accelerators and investors [LP §Sparkboot Business Plan, Executive Summary]. In Andrew's purpose
it restores "agency, dignity, and creativity" in the AI transition ([[life-purpose]]); in the Choices it is the Digital
Architect scene ([[choices-document]]).

How it earns is a locked decision in its own brain: a free first idea, then $5 / $10 for ten [sparkboot-brain,
finance/how-sparkboot-earns.md]. *(The Life Plan had $10 for ten; the brain's locked page governs.)*
